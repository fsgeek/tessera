## Legend

- **Status** values are `formal/PROPERTIES.md`'s (`open` → `modeling` → `checked` → `discharged`); "no TRK row" marks a registered claim the tracker does not list, which is itself an open cell.
- **Leg** cells name an artifact path or "none". A premise (a claim assumed in a header) is never a leg — rows 10 and 14 make this distinction explicit.
- **Join obligations** follow A3 §A3.3 conservation fields and ENUM §4: a TLA+ ↔ symbolic join is a ledger entry whose producer names the model and invariant; it is **never** marked symbolically discharged (the checkmark-relay defect, Sol finding 2). Two legs on one claim: their agreement is a checked obligation — checked so far: FL ↔ SP3 Q1(ii) (row 3), P5c ↔ P5P6 bridge (rows 5–6); unchecked: Accept ↔ P4 (row 1), P9 premise ↔ P9 model (row 10).
- **Red-bar condition** (ENUM Q2 ruling): two legs disagreeing on one claim. None observed to date; the cells where disagreement *could* appear once the missing legs exist are rows 1, 10, 11.
- **Open cells, counted**: rows 1, 2, 8 (no model on either assigned leg); 9 (no proof, no vectors); 10, 11 (no model on the assigned TLA+ leg); 13 (no model, exit-gating); 12, 14, 15 (no tracker row); 3 ([assumption] half); 4–7 (agreement-gate review not run; A3 model obligations untracked). The checked symbolic cell (row 3) cites P8 (row 9) as residual, and row 11's FL evidence (status `open`) routes its digest assumption to Layer 2 in the same direction; the checked TLA+ cells (rows 4–7) abstract bytes and signatures away and carry no P8 residual of their own — their dependence on P8 routes through rows 3/4, per the `P5P6_TemporalRevocation.tla` header (lines 23–25). *(Skeptic pass 2026-09-06: replaced "Every checked cell cites P8", which overclaimed the map's own contents.)*
- **Not in this map, deliberately**: the capstone (`formal/suite/capstone.pv`, unbuilt) is the instrument that discharges join obligations, not a claim; the integrated lifecycle model gates H1a, not Band 0; the conformance-vector extraction and informal written proof are tracker boxes, not component claims; nothing here restates an outcome probability or a prediction.
