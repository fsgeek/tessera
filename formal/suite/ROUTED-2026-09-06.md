**A6. P7's object-type enumeration lacks the terminal lineage record.**

**A7. Where the wrapper transplant of standing evidence is modelled.**
Source: `formal/suite/s-p7/PREDICTIONS.md` routed item 1 (sections
"Target" and "Boundary with S-STANDING", about 60 lines, optional);
`formal/suite/s-standing/PREDICTIONS.md` routed item 3. Panel criterion

*Recommendation:* S-STANDING carries the transplant with a minimal

**A8. Is the degraded-mode principle general?** Source:

route it again. *On no:* S-STANDING Q1d becomes a predicted defect of
