## 1. What is registered, and what the README promises

- **H2** (`docs/phase-0-prereg.md:225-231`): *"The architecture decisions are demonstrably mine; AI handled
  mechanics."* It is falsified only if the multi-model panel (the same ≥3 unrelated models as the security review,
  tasked to interrogate architecture ownership) *"finds a load-bearing decision I cannot justify without
  reconstructing it from the AI's reasoning."* Proposed A9 keeps this panel (A9:161-163).
- **The registered credibility line** (`docs/phase-0-prereg.md:86-89`): AI agents are *"not to _make_ those decisions
  without consultation."*
- **The README's stronger promise** (`README.md:13-17`): AI *"never contributes … ownership: every load-bearing
  decision is read cold by the author, ratified or overridden, and defensible by the author without the AI in the
  room."*

The README promise and H2 are different tests. This note keeps them apart.

## 3. Where each test stands — OWNER reading, contestable

- **The README promise: does not hold.** Load-bearing decisions have been made by the AI owner, and under proposed A9
  the author's signature on them records no objection, not ratification. This follows from the delegation alone.
- **H2: pending, not adjudicated.** Delegation and AI origin do not show that the author *cannot justify* a
  load-bearing decision; only the registered panel can find that. No panel has been run. The owner's expectation is
  that some post-delegation decisions would be hard for the author to defend unaided. That is a prediction, recorded
  here so it can be checked, not a finding.
- **4 December: product evidence only.** The relying-party test and the 2106-examiner test (`docs/V1-SCHEDULE.md:13-16`;
  A9:107-118) test whether V1's documentation and tools let one independent tester reach safe decisions on the
  registered mapping. They say nothing about who made the decisions or whether stewardship was enough. A pass cannot
  rehabilitate H2, and a fail does not falsify it.
- **The prototype.** `prototype/TRIAGE.md:74` reports 0 WRONG, 21 MISSING and 50 DEFER, with its stated limits:
  - exact model abstractions in stubbed areas;
  - the same model family as the spec (`:31-37`);
  - authority-channel scope assessed by analysis, not tested (`:96-97`).

  The author requested it, and AI built and analysed it. It is an implementation-facing check, not an independent
  one.

**Continuation rests on the accepted V1 schedule** and the author's ship-or-stop decision on 4 December
(`docs/V1-SCHEDULE.md:3,28`), not on any H2 outcome. For a different hypothesis, the prereg has an honest-stop clause:
*"If H0 cannot be met, the project does not proceed as designed — and that is a valid, honest outcome of this
pre-registration, not a failure to conceal"* (`:734-735`). It is cited here only as the project's stance on
reporting divergence; it is not an H2 rule.

## 4. Proposals, not rules

- **Run the H2 panel.** It is the registered adjudicator. It examines the author, so when to run it is his call.
  The owner's suggestion is after the 30 Oct freeze, when the V1 decision set is fixed.
- **Recording consideration.** When the author agrees with an owner recommendation after considering it, a sentence of
  his reason can be recorded alongside the OWNER label, keeping the original provenance. That documents
  consideration. It does not show comprehension and does not discharge H2.
- **Routing.**
  - The author receives in full, with links to the complete findings: findings that change direction, findings about
    authorship or credibility, and evidence against the owner's own conclusions.
  - Routine prover output and vectors are summarised, with links.
  - Judgments that A9 still reserves to the author (A9:216) stay his.
